Configure independent wheels and hotkeys for all eight action bars, with one wheel open at a time. Bar 1 uses fixed action slots 1-12 and starts unbound. Reusing a radial hotkey moves it to the new bar without changing other settings; cancelling or switching safely discards an old held release. Copy appearance, behavior, and placement between bars while keeping the destination's hotkey and actions.
